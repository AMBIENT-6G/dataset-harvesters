Capacitor charge time

Uses full measured voltage sweeps for the selected harvesters and frequency/source filters. Target-voltage chart filters do not limit the sweep.

Fits Pdc(V) = a·V² + b·V + c in watts using measured buffer voltages, with (0 V, 0 W) added as an anchor. Charge time sums C·(Vᵢ₊₁² − Vᵢ²)/(2·Pdc(Vmid)) over 1,000 voltage intervals, following Section III-A of the supplied paper. Defaults: 10 mF, 0.05 V to 1 V.

Estimates assume constant RF input power and no additional capacitor leakage or device load. The model spans the zero-voltage anchor through the largest measured voltage; unavailable ranges and nonpositive charging power are reported per series.
